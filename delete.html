Delete your Findfolk account and data

Last updated: 6 September 2026

You can delete your Findfolk account and related personal data at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account on our server.

Android package name: com.infinitelife.findfolk
iOS bundle identifier: com.infinitelife.joinhere

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Findfolk and sign in.
  2. Go to Me.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your account right away.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your Findfolk account:

Request account deletion

Or write to gracejackin@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Findfolk account and include:

  • The email address used to sign in (email, Google, or Sign in with Apple)
  • Your display name, if you remember it

We will verify the request and delete the account within 7 days. We may ask you to confirm from that same email address.

What we delete

  • Your profile (display name, email, timezone, sign-in identifiers)
  • Your room memberships
  • Your daily check-ins
  • Blocks you made or that applied to you

What may remain

  • Messages or photos you already posted in a room may stay as that group’s history, without your profile attached.
  • Rooms you hosted may stay if other members are still in them.
  • Anonymous analytics events are not tied to your email and are not used to identify you.
