Findfolk Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 8 September 2026

These standards apply to the mobile app Findfolk. They are published so anyone can read how we prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Android package name: com.infinitelife.findfolk
iOS bundle identifier: com.infinitelife.Findfolk

Findfolk is a task-based group app for adults. It is not a dating app, not a kids’ app, and not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly allow users under 18.

What we prohibit

Findfolk has zero tolerance for CSAE and CSAM. You must not create, share, request, store, or link to any of the following:

  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18, including images, video, text, or role-play
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexualization of minors
  • Child sexual abuse material, including fictional, AI-generated, or “barely legal” depictions of children
  • Links, invites, or instructions that lead to CSAE or CSAM off the app

This is banned even if the person claims to be 18, even in a closed room, and even as a “joke.”

How to report

In the app: open a room, use Report, or open Me and report an issue. Describe what you saw. We keep that report on the Findfolk server.

You can also email the child-safety contact: contact@bozhilianyi.cn (or gracejackin@gmail.com).

What we do after a report

  • We review the report and related messages, photos, and accounts.
  • We remove CSAE/CSAM and related rooms or messages when we have actual knowledge of them.
  • We can suspend or delete the accounts involved.
  • We preserve records needed to investigate and to comply with law.

Reporting to authorities

When we confirm CSAM, we report it to the appropriate authority. For the United States that includes the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC). In other regions we report to the relevant national or regional authority. We cooperate with lawful requests from those bodies.
